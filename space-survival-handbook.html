01 · DISPATCH

Welcome aboard

Long before our crew selection began, you'd already watched astronauts race around space and struggle with survival a doze time over, in cinemas, on laptops, half-paying attention on a plane. Without meaning to, these films built the shape of what you belive Mars survival looks like, long before mission planners ever explained what it actually takes.

This handbook exists to close the gap, where each section starts with a piece of science that is central to surviving the trasit and the planet itself. This is the same science thats embedded in the films you grew up on.

03 · INSTRUMENT

Radiation dose calculator

Drag to set your transit time and see what it actually costs you, based on dose rates measured by NASA's Curiosity rover (MSL/RAD instrument) en route to Mars.

How to read this: space is full of radiation that Earth's atmosphere normally blocks for us. Outside that protection, your body absorbs a small dose every single day of the trip, and those daily doses add up the longer the transit takes. The slider below sets how many days you spend travelling. The two boxes update to show your total absorbed dose, and roughly how many chest CT scans that's equivalent to, so the number feels concrete instead of abstract.
